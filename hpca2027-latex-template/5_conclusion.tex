\section{Conclusion}

FourShadow is an open-source, synthesizable tensor-core microarchitecture on the RISC-V Vortex GPGPU that answers two questions: the microarchitectural behavior behind NVIDIA's tensor-core features (Q1) and the behavior of a tensor core as its architectural parameters change (Q2). The key is that FourShadow is the white-box counterpart of a commercial tensor core: where the two agree, its visible mechanism is a candidate explanation; where they differ, the difference excludes one. We answer Q1 by measuring FourShadow beside the RTX 5080 and the H100, and Q2 by sweeping threads per warp and issue width, where per-lane warp-group MMA throughput peaks at 16 threads per warp. The same method extends to distributed shared memory and low-precision formats.
